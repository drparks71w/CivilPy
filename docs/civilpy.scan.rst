civilpy.scan package
====================

Scan-to-CAD: point-cloud segmentation, meshing, and feature extraction
for bridge sites. Streams multi-gigabyte LAS/LAZ scans through a voxel
grid, strips the ground, fits planes and cylinders, finds substructure
units and span lengths from the under-deck profile, measures per-span
under-clearance, and writes DXF / Rhino ``.3dm`` on the shared
``rhino_layers`` taxonomy.
